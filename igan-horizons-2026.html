IgAN Horizons 2026

IgAN Horizons is a free, one-day virtual educational conference hosted by the IgA Nephropathy Foundation for the IgA nephropathy community.

Date
Saturday, October 3, 2026
Time
9:00 AM–5:30 PM Eastern Time
Format
Virtual
Cost
Free

Who it is for

The Foundation describes IgAN Horizons as a resource for people who are newly diagnosed with IgA nephropathy, people living with IgAN, and caregivers. The official event page also says that live translation is available in more than 20 languages.

Why Tito is sharing it

Tito was diagnosed with IgA nephropathy. His book follows his personal journey from that diagnosis through kidney failure, dialysis, and transplantation. He is sharing this event because a patient-focused place to learn and connect may be useful to people at different points in their own journey.

Tito Needs a Kidney is an independent lived-experience site sharing this resource. Tito does not host, sponsor, medically endorse, or officially represent IgAN Horizons or the IgA Nephropathy Foundation.
