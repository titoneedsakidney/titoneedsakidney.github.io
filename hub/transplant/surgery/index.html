Transplant Surgery: Hospital Stay

The day of transplant surgery marks a major turning point in your kidney journey. This section explains what to expect during your hospital stay, introduces the surgical care team, and walks through immediate post-surgery recovery.

The Transplant Surgery

Learn how the surgery is performed, what happens during the operation, anesthesia details, and how the transplanted kidney is connected and begins working.

Surgical Staff Roles

Meet the transplant surgeons, anesthesiologists, surgical nurses, and other specialists who work together during your procedure.

In-Hospital Recovery

Understand what happens immediately after surgery—time in recovery rooms, pain management, monitoring, and early movement exercises to aid healing.

A successful transplant is a team effort. Knowing who’s involved and what to expect in the hospital helps you feel more comfortable and ready for this life-changing procedure.
