Tito Needs a Kidney

A Journey of Resilience, Community, and Hope

Tito Needs a Kidney is a firsthand account of kidney failure, dialysis, two kidney transplants, transplant loss, recovery, and learning how to keep living when the plan keeps changing.

The book tells the part that medical instructions often cannot: what the experience can feel like for a patient, how serious illness changes independence and relationships, and what it takes to keep moving forward through setbacks and uncertainty.
