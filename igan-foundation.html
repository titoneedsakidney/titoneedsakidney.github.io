IgA nephropathy resource

IgA Nephropathy Foundation

Tito was diagnosed with IgA nephropathy before progressing to kidney failure, dialysis, and transplantation. This page points readers to the IgA Nephropathy Foundation for current disease-specific education, support, patient tools, and community resources.

What the Foundation offers

The Foundation describes itself as a patient-centric organization focused on finding a cure for IgA nephropathy while supporting patients and caregivers. Its current resources include patient guides, treatment information, clinical-trial information, peer support, a nephrology provider finder, patient-aid information, and resources for coping, nutrition, caregivers, and life with IgAN.

Start with the official resources

  • IgA Nephropathy Foundation home — current Foundation programs, education, research, and news.
  • Tools and resources — guides, worksheets, treatment information, and resources organized around the patient journey.
  • Patient and care-partner support — peer-support options for people living with IgAN and those who support them.
  • Patient Aid — current eligibility and application information for the Foundation's patient-aid program.

Why this resource is here

IgA nephropathy is where Tito's kidney-disease story began. The memoir describes the lived experience of diagnosis, progression to kidney failure, dialysis, and transplant; the Foundation is the better place to go for current IgAN-specific programs and educational material.
