Tito Needs a Kidney

A Journey of Resilience, Community, and Hope

Tito Needs a Kidney is a candid firsthand account of kidney failure, dialysis, two kidney transplants, transplant loss, recovery, and the work of rebuilding a life around all of it.

It tells the parts of kidney disease that medical instructions often cannot: what treatment can feel like, how illness changes independence and relationships, and what it takes to keep moving forward when the plan falls apart.
